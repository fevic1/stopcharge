Privacy

No accounts. No first-party cookie wall. No list to sell.

What this site stores

Pages are static files on GitHub Pages. Letters on the templates page stay in your browser tab. We do not receive the text you type there.

What we do not do

  • No sign-in.
  • No payment form.
  • No email lookup of charges.
  • No first-party analytics pixel of our own.

What others may log

GitHub, your browser, and the network in between can see a normal page request. A public GitHub issue is public. Publisher mail to hello@stopcharge.net is read by Mbio Team. Do not send card numbers there.
