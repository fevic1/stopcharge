They charged you anyway

  1. 1 Cancel on the official path first.
  2. 2 Save three proofs: active plan, cancel confirmed, confirmation email file.
  3. 3 Write the merchant from your own inbox.
  4. 4 Ask for a refund. That is a request, not a guarantee.
  5. 5 If they ignore you, use the card app or PayPal. Your issuer’s rules control the window.
  6. 6 Still stuck: a consumer-protection desk in your country.

Many US card networks use a roughly 120-day window. PayPal’s published window is often longer, and some PayPal cases need an escalate inside about 20 days. Check the form in your bank or PayPal app.
