The cancel button is never where the signup button was.

Each page is the official path out, the fee they hope you miss, and a direct link. Fight back tools sit under that. They do not replace the cancel button. This site is a guide, not a merchant. Not a statement charge.

Amazon Prime

No cancel fee · refund window is short

Open Prime Central. Then End membership.

Netflix

Deleting the app does not cancel

Open cancel plan. Finish Cancellation. Billed by a partner? Use Apple or Play.

Disney Plus

Web cancel · bundles keep billing

Open account. Profile, Account, Cancel Subscription. Partner billed? Apple or Play.

Max

Max and HBO Max are the same bill

Open Max subscription. No Cancel button means a partner owns the receipt.

Spotify Premium

Often web only · partner plans hide Cancel

Open your plan. Cancel Premium. Family members cannot kill the manager’s bill.

YouTube Premium

YouTube, Play, or Apple. Match the receipt

Open paid memberships. iOS signup dies in Settings.

Adobe Creative Cloud

Annual plans can charge a fee after day 14

Open Adobe Plans. Manage plan → Cancel your plan.

Microsoft 365

Apps cannot cancel · storage drops after

Open Microsoft 365 services. Cancel subscription. Copy OneDrive files out first.

Dropbox

Cancel plan is a downgrade, not delete

Open Dropbox account. Cancel plan. Files above the free limit can lock.

ChatGPT Plus

Chat window cannot cancel

Open Settings, Billing. Apple or Play receipt? Cancel there.

Anything billed by Apple

The app cannot cancel itself

Before a bank chargeback, try reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple often grants a one time courtesy refund. A bank dispute can risk the Apple ID.

Phone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
Desktop: Apple subscriptions or apple.com/billing.

Anything billed by Google Play

The app is the wrong door

Open Play subscriptions.

LinkedIn Premium

No cancel fee · keep benefits until cycle ends

Me → Premium features → Manage subscription → Cancel. Apple or Google billed? Cancel there.

Audible

App will not cancel · credits expire

audible.com → Account details → Cancel membership. Finish every retention screen. Apple/Play billed? Cancel there.

They charged you anyway

Cancel first. Three screenshots. Then open your email client. Not a GitHub form, not a message to us.

  1. The active plan screen before you cancel.
  2. The cancellation confirmed screen with date and time.
  3. The confirmation email saved as a file.

Act fast: card disputes often die after 120 days. PayPal often 180. Escalate inside 20 days.

Copy letter or open your email client Full fight back guide

Letter to the company 
Subject: Refund. I cancelled, you charged me anyway

I cancelled [plan] on [date] through [website / Apple / Play]. I have the confirmation.
You still charged [amount] on [charge date]. Please refund that to the original payment method within 5 business days and confirm the plan will not renew.

I can send the three screenshots. If this stays unpaid I will dispute the charge with my bank.

[Name]
[Email already on the account]
Reason for the bank app 
Reason: cancelled recurring charge / services not received after cancel.

I cancelled [merchant / plan] on [cancel date] before this cycle.
They still took [amount] on [charge date]. I already asked them in writing for a refund.
Please credit this charge and stop further billing from this merchant.
Attached: active plan screenshot, cancel confirmed screenshot, confirmation email, my refund demand.

If they ignore the letter

  • Thailand: OCPB
