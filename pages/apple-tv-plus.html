How to cancel Apple TV Plus

Independent guide · not affiliated with Apple

Apple bills this plan. The TV app is the wrong door.

Last verified 4 Sep 2026

Open Apple subscriptions apple.com/billing

Phone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Open Apple TV Plus.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

On Apple TV hardware: Settings, Users and Accounts, your name, Subscriptions.

Desktop

Use Apple subscriptions or apple.com/billing.

Anything billed by Apple · Play subscriptions

The trap: cancel during a free trial can cut access at once. Before a bank dispute try reportaproblem.apple.com. A bank case can risk the Apple ID.
