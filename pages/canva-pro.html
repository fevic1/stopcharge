How to cancel Canva Pro

Cancel in Settings, Billing if Canva bills you. Uninstalling the app does not stop the plan. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Canva billing Official help

If Canva bills you

  1. Sign in to the right Canva account or team.
  2. Open your profile, then Settings, then Payments and plans, then Billing.
  3. Under the plan select Cancel plan, then Continue cancellation.
  4. Select Cancel subscription and keep the email.

On a team, only the owner or an administrator can cancel. A member will not see the button.

If you do not see Cancel plan

Apple or Google Play owns the bill. Cancel there. Pause is not cancel if you want the renewal to stop.
