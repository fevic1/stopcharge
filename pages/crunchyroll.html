How to cancel Crunchyroll

Independent guide · not affiliated with Crunchyroll

Cancel where you signed up. Deleting the app, logging out, or removing a card does not stop billing.

Last verified 27 Sep 2026

Open Membership Info Official help

If you subscribed on Crunchyroll.com (card or PayPal)

  1. Open a browser and go to crunchyroll.com. Log in and select your profile if asked.
  2. Click your profile icon → Settings → Membership Info.
  3. Choose Cancel Subscription.
  4. Click No thanks, Finish Cancellation. Optionally skip the feedback form.
  5. Keep the confirmation. You keep Premium until the end of the current billing period.

Source: official cancel guide (updated 15 Sep 2026).

If you signed up in the Crunchyroll app (Apple or Google)

The website will not show a Cancel button. Cancel inside the app or in the store that billed you:

  1. In the Crunchyroll app: Profile → Membership → Manage → Cancel Subscription → Continue with Cancellation, then finish in Apple or Google Play.
  2. Or go straight to the store: Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions.

If you signed up via Amazon, PlayStation, Roku, or YouTube

Crunchyroll Support cannot cancel partner bills. Open Membership Info on the Crunchyroll site; it will point you to the partner. Cancel there:

  • Amazon: manage subscriptions in your Amazon account
  • PlayStation: console or account management → subscriptions
  • Roku: account → manage subscriptions
  • YouTube Primetime: YouTube account → memberships

If you do not see Cancel

Common causes: wrong account, pending transaction (wait 24 hours), gift-card only (no cancel needed — it ends when the gift runs out), or a partner/store bill. Check Membership Info first. Still stuck: use the live chat on the help center (24/7 for account issues).

The trap: people delete the app or remove the card and assume the bill stops. It does not. The cancel path is always the original signup method. Pause is only available on Google Play; everywhere else you cancel and resubscribe later if needed.
