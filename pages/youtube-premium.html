How to cancel YouTube Premium

The receipt merchant decides the door. YouTube, Google Play, or Apple. Last checked 31 Aug 2026.

Open YouTube paid memberships Official help

Billed by YouTube / Google

  1. Open youtube.com/paid_memberships signed in.
  2. Open the membership → continue to cancel → confirm.
  3. You keep benefits until the period ends. Check for an end date on the same page.

Play-billed plans may bounce you to Play subscriptions.

Billed by Apple

You joined in the iOS app. Cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Apple subscriptions. YouTube cannot kill an Apple-billed plan.

Pause vs cancel

Some Google-billed plans offer a pause. A pause is not a cancel. If you want the renewal dead, finish Cancel.

The trap: YouTube Music Premium is often the same membership. Channel memberships are a different product. Match the line on the statement.
