How to cancel YouTube Premium

YouTube, Google Play, or Apple — match the receipt. Last checked 31 Aug 2026.

Open paid memberships Official help

Billed by YouTube / Google

Open youtube.com/paid_memberships → cancel. Play-billed plans may bounce to Play Store.

Billed by Apple

Cancel in Settings or Apple subscriptions. Ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com before a bank fight.
