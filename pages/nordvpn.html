How to cancel NordVPN

Independent guide · not affiliated with NordVPN

Turn auto-renewal off in your Nord Account. Deleting the app does not cancel the bill.

Last verified 12 Sep 2026

Open Nord Account Official help

If you bought directly from NordVPN

  1. Sign in at my.nordaccount.com.
  2. Open the Billing tab in the left menu.
  3. Find your NordVPN plan. Next to Auto-renewal, click Cancel.
  4. Confirm by selecting Cancel auto-renewal.
  5. Status changes to Off. You should receive a confirmation email.

Service continues until the end of the paid period. Same steps work for NordPass, NordLocker, Dedicated IP, or bundles.

Source: official cancel auto-renewal help.

If you bought through Apple, Google Play, or Amazon

NordVPN cannot cancel store bills. Cancel where you subscribed:

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

Amazon Appstore: manage subscriptions in your Amazon account.

Refund window

30-day money-back guarantee on the initial purchase only (not renewals, unless local law requires). After you turn off auto-renewal, contact support via live chat at support.nordvpn.com or email support@nordvpn.com and request the refund. They send a link to submit it.

The trap: long prepaid plans (1–2 years) auto-renew by default. People uninstall the app and assume billing stopped. The cancel lives only in the Nord Account billing tab, not inside the VPN client. Save the confirmation email and a screenshot of Auto-renewal: Off.
