How to cancel Microsoft 365

Cancel in the Microsoft account if Microsoft billed you. Word and Excel cannot cancel the plan. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Microsoft 365 services Official help

Personal or Family, billed by Microsoft

  1. Sign in at account.microsoft.com/services/microsoft365.
  2. Select Cancel subscription (it may say Upgrade or Cancel).
  3. If you only see Turn on recurring billing, the plan already ends on the date shown.
  4. Choose that you do not want the subscription, then keep the email.

Work or school tenants cancel in the Microsoft 365 admin center, not this page.

If you do not see Cancel

Apple or Google Play owns the bill. Cancel there. A bank dispute against Apple can risk the Apple ID. Try reportaproblem.apple.com first.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The trap: after cancel, OneDrive storage often drops to 5 GB. Copy files out before the paid term ends.
