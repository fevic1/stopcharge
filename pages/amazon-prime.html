How to cancel Amazon Prime

No phone call. Several screens designed to keep you. Last checked 31 Aug 2026.

Open Prime Central

On the website

  1. Open Prime Central.
  2. Manage membership → End membership.
  3. Finish every confirmation screen.

If you subscribed through Apple or Google

Apple or Play. Amazon cannot end a store-billed plan.

The trap: Pause is not cancel. Cancel usually means keep benefits until the paid period ends.
