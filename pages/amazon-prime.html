How to cancel Amazon Prime

No phone call. No cancel fee. Several screens designed to keep you. Last checked 30 Aug 2026.

Open Prime Central (amazon.com)

Use your local Amazon host if that is where you pay (amazon.co.uk/gp/primecentral, amazon.sg, …).

On the website

  1. Sign in, then open Prime Central.
  2. Under Manage membership choose Update, cancel, and more → End membership.
  3. Keep going through Cancel my benefits / Continue to cancel until it is confirmed.

In the Amazon Shopping app

  1. Profile → Account → Memberships & subscriptions → Prime.
  2. Manage membership → End membership.
  3. Finish the confirmation screens. Benefits usually run until the paid period ends.

If you subscribed through Apple or Google

Cancel in Apple Subscriptions or Google Play. Amazon cannot end a store-billed plan from its own site.

The trap: Cancel often means keep benefits until the period ends, not refund today. A full refund is typically only if you have not used Prime benefits since the last charge, or within a short signup window. Pause is not cancel. Screenshot the confirmation email.
