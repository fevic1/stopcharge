How to cancel Amazon Prime

No phone call. No fee. Several screens designed to keep you. Last checked 30 Aug 2026.

On the website

  1. Sign in at amazon.com (or your local Amazon).
  2. Hover Account & Lists → Your Prime membership.
  3. Open Manage membership.
  4. Choose End membership, then keep clicking through the offers until you confirm.

In the Amazon Shopping app

  1. Tap the profile / menu, then Prime or Account → Prime membership.
  2. Manage membership → End membership.
  3. Finish the confirmation screens. Benefits usually run until the paid period ends.

If you subscribed through Apple or Google

Cancel in Apple Subscriptions or Google Play. Amazon cannot end a store-billed plan from its own site.

The trap: Cancel often means keep benefits until the period ends, not refund today. A full refund is typically only if you have not used Prime benefits since the last charge, or within a short signup window. Screenshot the confirmation email.

Sources: Amazon Prime membership help. Menus move. If a button is missing, search Amazon help for End Your Amazon Prime Membership.
