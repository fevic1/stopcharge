How to cancel Hulu

Cancel on the Hulu account page if Hulu bills you. Pause is not cancel. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Hulu account Official help

If Hulu bills you

  1. Sign in at the Hulu account page in a browser.
  2. Under Your Subscription select Cancel.
  3. If they offer pause, select Continue to Cancel.
  4. Select Cancel Subscription and keep the email.

The account page should then say the subscription will be canceled. You keep access until the paid term ends. Cancel is not delete.

If you do not see Cancel

Check Payment Information on that page. Apple, Play, Amazon, or a Disney bundle partner owns the bill. Cancel there.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions · Disney Plus

The trap: pause keeps the account in a holding pattern. Next Charge on the overview means you did not finish cancel.
