How to cancel 23andMe Premium

Independent guide · not affiliated with 23andMe

Cancel auto-renewal in Account Settings. Deleting the app or closing the browser does not stop the bill.

Last verified 25 Sep 2026

Open Account Settings Official help

If 23andMe bills you directly

  1. Log in to your 23andMe account.
  2. Click your profile name in the upper-right corner and select Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Memberships section and click Manage.
  4. In Manage membership, select Cancel auto-renewal.
  5. Wait for the confirmation email titled “Your membership will not automatically renew.” Save it. It states the date your billing cycle ends.

You keep Premium access until the end of the current billing cycle. After that you lose premium reports and features but keep basic reports. Premium memberships are non-refundable and cannot be prorated.

If you bought through Apple or Google Play

23andMe cannot cancel a store bill. Cancel where you subscribed:

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

Account closure (separate from cancel)

Canceling Premium only stops the membership renewal. To delete the entire account and data, use Account Settings → request account closure. That process is permanent and cannot be reversed once confirmed.

The trap: auto-renew is on by default and past complaints describe charges after people thought they had turned it off. Always wait for the confirmation email. Uninstalling the app never cancels. No prorated refunds.
