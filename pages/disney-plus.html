How to cancel Disney Plus

Cancel on the web if Disney bills you. Deleting the app does not stop the charge. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Disney Plus account Official help

On the web

  1. Sign in at Disney Plus in a browser.
  2. Open Profile, then Account.
  3. Open the Disney Plus plan under Subscription.
  4. Select Cancel Subscription and keep the confirmation.

You keep access until the end of the paid term. Cancel is not the same as deleting the account. Cancel first.

If you do not see Cancel

The receipt merchant is not Disney. Cancel with Apple, Google Play, a carrier, or the bundle partner (Hulu, ESPN, Max) named on the statement.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The trap: a bundle plan can keep billing after you cancel only one service. Read the receipt.
