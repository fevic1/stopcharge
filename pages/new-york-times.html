How to cancel New York Times

Independent guide · not affiliated with The New York Times

Cancel digital or home delivery in Account when eligible. Otherwise chat or call. Deleting the app does not stop the bill.

Last verified 18 Sep 2026

Open NYT cancel Official help

If billed by The New York Times (eligible plans)

  1. Sign in at myaccount.nytimes.com.
  2. Open Subscription Overview.
  3. Under Manage Subscription, select Cancel your Subscription.
  4. Follow the prompts. Decline any retention offers and confirm.
  5. Keep the confirmation email.

Or go directly to nytimes.com/account/cancel.

Source: official Cancel Your Subscription help.

Chat or phone (when the button is missing)

Chat: open the official help page and select Contact Customer Care / Contact us at the bottom.

Phone (US): 866-273-3612. Hours: 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET Monday–Friday; 7 a.m. to 2 p.m. ET weekends and holidays. (Some pages list slightly longer hours; confirm on the live help page.)

International numbers are listed on the NYT help site.

If you bought through Apple, Google Play, or The Athletic

NYT cannot cancel store or partner bills.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The Athletic: cancel inside Athletic account settings.

The trap: pause is not cancel. Retention screens offer discounts or shorter pauses. App uninstall or signing out leaves the subscription live. Third-party (Apple/Google) bills require canceling at the store, not on nytimes.com.
