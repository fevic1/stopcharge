How to cancel HelloFresh

Independent guide · not affiliated with HelloFresh

Cancel on the web under Account Settings. Skipping a week is not the same as ending the plan.

Last verified 5 Sep 2026

Open HelloFresh Official help

On the web (US)

  1. Sign in at hellofresh.com.
  2. Click your name (top right) → Account Settings.
  3. On Plan settings, scroll to Status and click Cancel Plan.
  4. Follow the prompts. Decline pause or discount offers if you want a full exit.
  5. Keep the confirmation message and email.

Cutoff: cancel or skip by 11:59 pm PST five days before the next scheduled delivery. After that cutoff the next box is already processed and will bill.

Skip is not cancel

My Menu → pick the week → Skip Week only pauses that delivery. The subscription stays live and will charge the next weeks unless you cancel the plan.

Other regions

UK, CA, and other markets use similar Account Settings / Cancel steps on their local HelloFresh domain. Always use the site for the country that bills you.

The trap: retention offers (pause or discount) and the 5-day cutoff. Miss the cutoff and the next box ships and charges even if you cancel later.
