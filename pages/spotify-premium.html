How to cancel Spotify Premium

Independent guide · not affiliated with Spotify

Use a browser on the account page.

Last verified 4 Sep 2026

Open Spotify plan Official help

If Spotify bills you directly

  1. Open Manage your plan in a browser.
  2. Select Cancel subscription / Cancel Premium.
  3. Premium runs to the next billing date, then the account goes free. Playlists stay.

If you do not see Cancel

Carrier, Apple, or Google Play is the merchant. Apple · Play.

Family or Duo

Only the plan manager cancels the paid plan.
