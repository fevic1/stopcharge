How to cancel Spotify Premium

Use a browser on the account page. Family members cannot kill the manager’s plan. Last checked 31 Aug 2026.

Open Spotify plan Official help

If Spotify bills you directly

  1. Open Manage your plan in a browser.
  2. Select Cancel subscription / Cancel Premium.
  3. Confirm. Premium runs to the next billing date, then the account goes free. Playlists stay.

If you do not see Cancel

Check Payment on the account page. Carrier, Apple, or Google Play is the merchant. Cancel there, not in the Spotify app alone.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

Family or Duo

Only the plan manager cancels the paid plan. A member who hits cancel only leaves the family. Tell the manager.

The trap: the mobile app is the wrong door for many accounts. If Cancel is missing, you are on a partner plan.
