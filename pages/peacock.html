How to cancel Peacock

Cancel in Plans and Payments if Peacock bills you. There is no pause. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Peacock account Official help

If Peacock bills you

  1. Sign in at the Peacock account page.
  2. Open Plans and Payments.
  3. Select Cancel All Subscriptions or Cancel Plan.
  4. Keep the on screen message and the email.

That stops the base plan and add ons such as a regional sports network or Starz. To drop only an add on, open that add on and remove it.

If you do not see Cancel

Apple, Play, Roku, Amazon, or a carrier owns the bill. Peacock cannot cancel that plan. Use the partner named on the receipt.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The trap: unused days are often not refunded unless the law in your country requires it. Access runs to the end of the paid term.
