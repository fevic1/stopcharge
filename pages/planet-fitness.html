How to cancel Planet Fitness

Home club only. Written notice or in person for most locations. Online for some. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Official membership FAQs Member login

1. Try online first

Log in at planetfitness.com (or the PF app) and look under membership / account options for Cancel. Availability depends on your home club location and membership type. Screenshot everything.

2. In person at your home club

  1. Go to the club where you signed up (your home club — not any other location).
  2. Bring photo ID.
  3. Ask for the cancellation form, fill it, sign it.
  4. Get a dated copy or photo of the completed form before you leave.

3. Written notice by mail

If you cannot visit, send a signed letter to the street address of your home club. Use certified mail with return receipt. Include your full name, member ID / Club Pass number, address, and a clear statement that you cancel effective immediately (or on a date). Keep the receipt.

The trap: Notice must arrive by the 10th to stop the next monthly draft (they process up to 7 business days). Annual fee needs notice by the 25th of the prior month. Early exit on a minimum-term contract can cost a $58 buyout. Phone and plain email are not accepted at most clubs.
