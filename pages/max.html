How to cancel Max

Max and HBO Max are the same bill. Cancel with whoever is on the receipt. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Max subscription HBO Max subscription

If Max or HBO bills you

  1. Sign in at max.com/subscription.
  2. Read the biller at the top of the page.
  3. Select Cancel Your Subscription.
  4. Confirm and save the email.

Deleting the app does not cancel. You usually keep the service until the paid period ends.

If you do not see Cancel

Apple, Play, Amazon, Roku, Samsung, or an internet provider owns the bill. Cancel there. A sports add on can sit on a different biller than the base plan.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The trap: the site shows no Cancel when a partner bills you. The statement line is the door.
