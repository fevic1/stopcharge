How to cancel Amazon Music

Unlimited and Standard have their own cancel. Music that comes with Prime ends only when Prime ends. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Amazon Music settings Prime cancel

Unlimited or Standard, billed by Amazon

  1. Sign in at Amazon Music settings.
  2. Open Subscription Renewal.
  3. Select Cancel and confirm.
  4. Keep the confirmation.

You can also use Account and Lists, then Memberships and Subscriptions, then Cancel next to Amazon Music. Alexa can cancel some Standard plans if Amazon billed them.

If you do not see Cancel

Apple or Play owns the Unlimited bill. Cancel there. If the only music perk is Prime, end Prime instead.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions · Amazon Prime

The trap: ending Unlimited does not end Prime. Ending Prime does not always end a paid Unlimited add on. Read the line on the statement.
