How to cancel Paramount Plus

Cancel on the web if Paramount bills you. Apple, Play, Amazon, Roku, or a carrier will hide the button. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Paramount Plus account Official help

If Paramount bills you

  1. Sign in at Paramount Plus in a browser.
  2. Open your username, then Account.
  3. Select Cancel subscription and finish the prompts.
  4. Keep the confirmation.

Smart TV and console signups that billed Paramount still cancel on this account page, not inside the TV app.

If you do not see Cancel

Apple, Play, Amazon, Roku, Walmart Plus, or a phone carrier owns the bill. Cancel there. The Paramount site cannot stop that charge.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The trap: unused days are often not refunded. Access usually runs to the next renewal, then stops.
