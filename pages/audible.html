How to cancel Audible

App will not cancel. Use the website. Credits expire on cancel. Last checked 31 Aug 2026.

Open Audible Official cancel help

If Audible / Amazon bills you

  1. Sign in at audible.com in a browser (desktop or mobile web).
  2. Select your name → Account details / Membership details.
  3. Select Cancel membership.
  4. Click Continue through every retention screen until the final cancel confirmation.

You keep titles bought with credits or cash. Unused credits usually expire when the plan ends.

If Apple or Google bills you

Audible cannot cancel a store-billed plan. Apple · Play.

The trap: The Audible app has no cancel path. Pause is not cancel — it auto-restarts. Retention pages hide the real Cancel link in small text. Use remaining credits before you finish cancel.
