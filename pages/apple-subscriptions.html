How to cancel anything billed by Apple

The app that signed you up is the wrong door. Cancel stops the next bill. A refund for a charge that already posted is a separate request. Last checked 31 Aug 2026.

Request an Apple refund Subscriptions apple.com/billing

Apple’s own refund door

Official path: Apple Support — Request a refund.

  1. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple ID on the receipt.
  2. Choose I’d like to → Request a refund.
  3. Pick a reason, pick the charge, submit.
  4. Apple says to wait 24–48 hours for an update. Approved money returns to the original method, then the bank’s clock.

Cancel does not auto-refund a charge that already posted. Do both: cancel the plan, then request the refund. Family organizer can request for a Family Sharing purchase. Search mail for “receipt from Apple” if the charge is missing on the page.

Cancel the renewal

Phone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → the plan → Cancel.

Desktop: Apple subscriptions or apple.com/billing.

Cancel more than 24 hours before renewal. The company app usually cannot kill an Apple-billed plan.

Bank chargeback last. A dispute filed against Apple can lock or limit the Apple ID (“billing problem with previous purchase”). Use Report a Problem first. If Apple refuses and the charge is still unauthorized after cancel, then use the bank — and keep the Apple screenshots.
