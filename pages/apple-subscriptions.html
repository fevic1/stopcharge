How to cancel anything billed by Apple

The app that signed you up is the wrong door. Apple keeps the list in Settings. Last checked 30 Aug 2026.

Open Apple subscriptions apple.com/billing Apple cancel help

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Open the service → Cancel Subscription.

Mac or browser

  1. Open Apple subscriptions or apple.com/billing.
  2. Sign in with the Apple ID on the receipt.
  3. Find the plan → Cancel.

Mac App Store path: your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions → Manage.

No Apple device

Same two links above, or account.apple.com. The company app still cannot cancel an Apple-billed plan.

The trap: cancelling inside the company app often does nothing if Apple is the merchant. Family Sharing can hide a plan under another Apple ID. Cancel more than 24 hours before renewal.

If they still take the money

Evidence + dispute steps · Refund letter

Apple-billed plans still have to die in Settings or those Apple pages. Then freeze a virtual card for any direct merchant bill. US: privacy.com.
