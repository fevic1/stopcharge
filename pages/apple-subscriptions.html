How to cancel anything billed by Apple

The app that signed you up is the wrong door. Apple keeps the list in Settings. Last checked 31 Aug 2026.

Open Apple subscriptions apple.com/billing Report a problem

Before a bank chargeback

Open reportaproblem.apple.com first. Apple sometimes issues a one-time courtesy refund. A bank dispute against Apple can put the Apple ID at risk.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Open the service → Cancel Subscription.

Mac or browser

  1. Open Apple subscriptions or apple.com/billing.
  2. Sign in with the Apple ID on the receipt.
  3. Find the plan → Cancel.

The trap: cancelling inside the company app often does nothing if Apple is the merchant. Family Sharing can hide a plan under another Apple ID. Cancel more than 24 hours before renewal.
