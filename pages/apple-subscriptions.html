How to cancel anything billed by Apple

The app that signed you up is the wrong door. Apple keeps the list in Settings. Last checked 30 Aug 2026.

Open Apple subscriptions Apple cancel help

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Open the service → Cancel Subscription.

Mac

  1. Open the App Store.
  2. Click your name / account → Account Settings.
  3. Find Subscriptions → Manage → cancel.

No Apple device

Sign in at account.apple.com and follow the subscription prompts, or use the subscriptions link above on a browser that can sign in to Apple.

The trap: cancelling inside the company app often does nothing if Apple is the merchant. Family Sharing can hide a plan under another Apple ID. Cancel more than 24 hours before renewal.

Apple-billed plans still have to die in Settings. Then freeze a virtual card for any direct merchant bill. US: privacy.com.
