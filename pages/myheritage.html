How to cancel MyHeritage

Independent guide · not affiliated with MyHeritage

Web purchases cancel under My purchases. App Store and Play purchases cancel in the store. Deleting the app does not stop the bill.

Last verified 30 Sep 2026

Open MyHeritage Official help

If MyHeritage bills you directly

  1. Sign in at myheritage.com on a browser.
  2. Click your name in the upper right and open My purchases.
  3. Under My purchases, open Complete subscriptions, Site subscriptions, or Data subscriptions.
  4. Find the live plan and the next billing date. Click Cancel my subscription.
  5. Click through offers until you confirm Stop automatic renewal.
  6. Save the confirmation screen or email.

Access usually continues until the current period ends. A greyed-out button often means renewal is already off.

If you bought through Apple or Google Play

MyHeritage cannot cancel store bills. Official help: app purchase cancel article.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

If you cannot log in

Use the official contact path: myheritage.com/contact-myheritage. Ask to stop automatic renewal. Include the account email and a payment last-four if you have it. Do not send full card numbers.

Refund requests are a separate official flow under Help → Contact Us → Billing & Charges → Request a refund. Attach proof of payment if you cannot find the account.

The trap: site vs data vs photo plans can each renew. Cancel every live line under My purchases. Uninstalling MyHeritage or Reimagine never cancels. Retention screens are not a cancel.
