How to cancel Adobe Creative Cloud

Official door: account.adobe.com/plans. Annual-paid-monthly plans charge a fee after day 14. Last checked 30 Aug 2026.

If you bought from Adobe

  1. Sign in at account.adobe.com/plans.
  2. On the plan, select Manage plan.
  3. Select Cancel your plan.
  4. If that button is missing, Adobe says a payment is still processing — wait 24 hours.
  5. Continue to cancel → pick a reason → Confirm cancellation.
  6. Check email and the same plans page. Each plan must be cancelled separately.

If you bought through Apple, Google, or Microsoft

Adobe cannot cancel it. Use Apple or Google Play.

The trap: Adobe terms: annual plan paid monthly can charge an early-termination fee of 50% of the remaining contract after 14 days. Month-to-month is cleaner. Apps keep working until the current paid period ends.

If they still take the money

Lock a virtual card to Adobe and freeze it. Official cancel first.
