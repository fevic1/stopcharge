How to cancel LinkedIn Premium

No cancel fee. Access until the billing cycle ends. Cancel at least one day before renewal. Last checked 30 Aug 2026.

Official LinkedIn cancel help Premium products / manage

If you bought directly from LinkedIn (desktop)

  1. Sign in at linkedin.com.
  2. Click the Me icon at the top → Premium features.
  3. Click Manage subscription (or Subscription details → Manage subscription).
  4. You land in LinkedIn Admin Center → Purchases.
  5. Select the active subscription → Cancel subscription under Actions.
  6. Click through the retention screens → Confirm cancel.

Android app also works for direct purchases. iOS app often does not — use desktop.

If you bought through Apple App Store

Cancel in Apple Subscriptions (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or via the LinkedIn iOS app path if shown. LinkedIn cannot refund Apple-billed plans.

If you bought through Google Play

Cancel in Google Play subscriptions or on LinkedIn desktop / Android app.

The trap: Several “are you sure / keep the benefits” screens. Features stay until the current cycle ends — no same-day refund for ordinary cancels. Refund window is typically 7 days from charge if you have not used Premium features (see LinkedIn refund policy). Restricted accounts sometimes block self-serve cancel — contact support. US and Germany sometimes allow cancel without full login.

If they still take the money

Evidence + dispute steps · Refund letter

Then freeze a virtual card locked to LinkedIn. US: privacy.com. Elsewhere: Revolut / Wise / bank virtual card.
