How to cancel David Lloyd Clubs

Independent guide · not affiliated with David Lloyd Leisure

Notice has to be in writing. Cancelling the Direct Debit does not end the membership. Standard plans run at least 12 full calendar months.

Last verified 2 Oct 2026

Open help centre Official membership terms

Give written notice

  1. Check the membership type on the agreement or in the David Lloyd Clubs app. Standard is at least 12 full calendar months. Flexible is at least 3.
  2. Send notice in writing. Official terms (A2) accept the David Lloyd Clubs app, email, post, or handing it in at the home club.
  3. Club email pattern published on the official site: clubname@davidlloyd.co.uk (example they give: Aberdeen@davidlloyd.co.uk). Include full name, home club, and membership number.
  4. Post, if you use it, to the registered office on the Direct Debit page: David Lloyd Leisure Ltd, The Hangar, Mosquito Way, Hatfield Business Park, Hatfield, Hertfordshire, AL10 9AX.
  5. Get proof they received it. Terms say ask for a club receipt, an email delivery receipt, or the app’s automated receipt. Notice is not effective until they have received it.

Member contact on the site points to the app chat, not a public cancel button. There is no official one-click end-membership URL.

How long you still pay

  1. Help centre: standard memberships run at least 12 full calendar months, then continue until you say you want to end them. Flexible runs at least 3 full calendar months, then continues.
  2. After the initial period, standard needs at least three full calendar months’ written notice. Flexible needs one full calendar month.
  3. To finish exactly at the end of the initial period, terms say notice must be received by the first day of the final calendar month. Miss that and they treat it as three more months from the next month (standard).
  4. Calendar-month rule (A2): notice received after the 4th of a month counts from the 1st of the next month. Notice received on the 1st through the 4th counts from the 1st of that month.
  5. Keep paying until the end date they confirm. Terms say cancelling the Direct Debit during the minimum term does not end the commitment.

14-day cancel, if you joined at a distance

Terms section A5: if you applied online, on the phone, or by an app, you can cancel the whole membership up to 14 days after the application. If anyone uses the club in that window, they charge a proportion of the monthly fee and refund the rest. In-club sign-up does not get that distance-selling window. A local 14-day money-back promo is a club offer, not the membership rule.

The trap: the expensive plan is often the standard 12-month commitment, not a monthly exit. Stopping the Direct Debit is not notice. Suspension is not ending the membership. Notice after the 4th slips a whole month. Family or linked members: the lead member’s notice covers linked people unless you say otherwise.

Source: David Lloyd Clubs, UK Terms and Conditions of Membership, December 2022 (A2 notice, A4 length, A5 14-day distance cancel, A14 ending), plus help centre membership length and club email pattern, checked 2 Oct 2026. Fresh complaint 1 Oct 2026: a member who picked the expensive plan and could not leave for 12 months.
