How to cancel ChatGPT Plus

Cancel in Settings, Billing if OpenAI billed you. The chat window is the wrong door. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open ChatGPT settings Official help

If you paid on chatgpt.com

  1. Sign in at ChatGPT.
  2. Open the account menu, then Settings, then Billing.
  3. Under Cancel plan, select Cancel.
  4. Keep the confirmation.

Business workspaces cancel under Workspace settings, Billing, Manage plan. Enterprise and Edu are not this page.

If you do not see Cancel plan

Apple or Google Play owns the bill. Cancel there. Two stores plus the web can mean two charges. Match the receipt first.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The trap: deleting the chat history does not stop Plus. Accidental web charges are often only refundable if you ask OpenAI inside 14 days.
