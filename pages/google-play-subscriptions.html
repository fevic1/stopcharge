How to cancel anything billed by Google Play

The app is decoration. Play Store owns the charge. Last checked 30 Aug 2026.

On the phone

  1. Open the Play Store.
  2. Tap your profile photo.
  3. Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  4. Open the service → Cancel subscription.

On the web

Sign in at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

The trap: some apps show a fake cancel that only emails support. If the charge is on your Google statement, only this list ends it. Pause is not cancel.

Source: Google Play Help Cancel, pause, or change a subscription.
