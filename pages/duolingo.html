How to cancel Super Duolingo

Independent guide · not affiliated with Duolingo

Cancel on the platform that took the payment. Web, Apple, and Google Play are different bills. Deleting the app does not stop the charge.

Last verified 1 Oct 2026

Open Super settings Official help

If you subscribed on the web

  1. Sign in at duolingo.com in a browser.
  2. Click your profile picture and open Settings.
  3. Open Super Duolingo.
  4. Select Cancel subscription and confirm.
  5. Save the confirmation screen. Official help says canceling does not refund or prorate a charge already taken.

Direct settings URL published by Duolingo: duolingo.com/settings/super.

If you subscribed in the Duolingo app

  1. Open the Duolingo app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Super icon at the top of the home screen.
  3. Tap Settings (gear), then Manage subscription.
  4. Select Cancel subscription.
  5. Confirm in Google Play, the App Store, or Alipay. The store owns the receipt.

If you started it in phone settings instead of the app, Duolingo points Android users to a support request and iOS users to Apple. Store bills: Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions.

Refund is a separate request

Official help: charges are generally non-refundable, and there is no credit for a partly used period. Apple refunds go to Apple, not Duolingo. Web and Android refund requests use Duolingo support and are a review, not a guarantee. Article: How do I request a refund?

The trap: deleting the app or the account does not cancel Super. Official help says so twice. A rejected refund is not the same as a canceled renewal. Family Plan is managed only by the family manager. Match the door to the statement line (DUOLINGO vs APPLE.COM/BILL vs GOOGLE *).

Source: Duolingo Help, “How do I cancel my Super Duolingo subscription?”, checked 1 Oct 2026. Fresh complaint the same day: a buyer whose Duolingo refund was rejected after purchase.
