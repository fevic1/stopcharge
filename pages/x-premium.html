How to cancel X Premium

Cancel on the platform you used to subscribe. Features last until the cycle ends. No refund for time already paid. Last checked 3 Sep 2026.

Official Premium FAQ Open X Premium

Subscribed on the web (x.com / Stripe)

  1. Log in at x.com.
  2. Go to Settings and privacy → Premium (or open x.com/i/premium).
  3. Manage subscription → Cancel plan (or Cancel).
  4. Confirm. Screenshot the confirmation.

Web cancellations go through Stripe. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

Subscribed through Apple

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → X → Cancel Subscription. Or use Apple subscriptions.

Subscribed through Google Play

Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → X → Cancel. Or use Play subscriptions.

The trap: Uninstalling the app or deactivating your account does not cancel billing. You must cancel on the exact platform that holds the receipt. Refunds are not available except where required by law. Blue checkmark and features stay until the paid period ends.
