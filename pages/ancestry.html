How to cancel Ancestry

Independent guide · not affiliated with Ancestry

Cancel on the web under Account Settings. Deleting the app or trees does not stop the bill.

Last verified 6 Sep 2026

Open Account Settings Official help

If Ancestry bills you directly

  1. Sign in at ancestry.com/account.
  2. In the Membership section, find the plan and click Cancel membership or Cancel trial.
  3. Choose cancel (not pause or keep). Select a reason if asked.
  4. Click through any offers until you see Cancel my membership.
  5. Wait for the confirmation number and email. Print or save it.

You keep access until the current period ends, then the account becomes a free registered guest. Trees and DNA results stay unless you delete the account.

If you bought through Apple, Google Play, or Amazon

Ancestry cannot cancel store bills. Cancel where you subscribed:

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

Multiple accounts or family plan

Only the original purchaser can cancel a family plan. If you still get charged after canceling one membership, check for a second Ancestry account or contact Ancestry support to locate other accounts.

The trap: retention offers and the pause option. Keep clicking until you receive a cancellation confirmation number. Uninstalling the app never cancels.
