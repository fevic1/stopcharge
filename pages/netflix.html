How to cancel Netflix

Cancel on the web. Signing out or deleting the app does not stop the bill. Last checked 31 Aug 2026.

Open Netflix cancel plan Official help

On the web

  1. Sign in at Netflix.
  2. Open netflix.com/cancelplan.
  3. Tap or click Cancel, then Finish Cancellation.
  4. Keep the confirmation email.

You keep watching until the end of the paid period. You are not charged again unless the plan is restarted.

If you do not see Cancel

The receipt merchant is not Netflix. Look under Membership on the account page. Cancel with Apple, Google Play, your carrier, or the partner listed there.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The trap: someone else on the household can restart the plan. After cancel, change the password and sign out of all devices if the charge returns.
