How to cancel Norton

Independent guide · not affiliated with Norton

Stop auto-renewal in your Norton account. Deleting the app does not cancel the bill.

Last verified 4 Sep 2026

Open My Subscriptions Official help

If you bought from Norton

  1. Sign in at my.norton.com (My Subscriptions).
  2. Find the plan → Cancel Subscription Renewal or Manage Renewal.
  3. Next to Plan Renewal, click Unsubscribe.
  4. Pick a reason. When offers appear, choose No thanks, cancel my subscription.
  5. Wait for the confirmation. Changes can take a few hours (up to 24).

Your protection keeps running until the paid term ends. After that it stops.

If you bought through Apple, Google Play, or Microsoft

Norton cannot cancel store bills. Cancel where you subscribed:

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

Refund window

Annual plans: full refund request often available within 60 days of charge (check current policy). Monthly: shorter window, often 14 days on first purchase. Contact support after you stop renewal if you need a refund.

The trap: retention screens and discounts. Click through to the final “No thanks, cancel.” Also delete the stored payment method if you want no future re-activation risk.
