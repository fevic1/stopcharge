How to cancel Washington Post

Independent guide · not affiliated with The Washington Post

Cancel digital-only in My Post when billed by WaPo. Deleting the app or signing out does not stop the bill.

Last verified 19 Sep 2026

Open My Post subscription Official help

If billed directly by The Washington Post (digital)

  1. Sign in at washingtonpost.com.
  2. Open My Post → Subscription.
  3. Find your plan and choose Manage or Cancel / Cancel subscription.
  4. Follow the prompts. Confirm cancellation.
  5. Keep the confirmation email or screenshot.

When you cancel, charges stop for the next billing cycle. Access continues until the end of the current paid period.

Source: official How to cancel your digital-only subscription.

Add-on subscriptions

Cancel add-ons the same way: My Post → Subscription & Billing → Manage or Cancel next to the add-on.

If you bought through Apple, Google Play, Amazon, or Google News

The Washington Post cannot cancel store or partner bills.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

Amazon: use Amazon Memberships & Subscriptions. Google News: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions or myaccount.google.com/subscriptions.

Print / home delivery

Manage in My Post when linked, or contact Home Delivery Customer Care. Phone listed on WaPo help for combination plans is often 202-334-6100.

The trap: app uninstall or signing out leaves the subscription live. Third-party (Apple/Google/Amazon) bills require canceling at the store, not on washingtonpost.com. No refund for the current period in most cases.
