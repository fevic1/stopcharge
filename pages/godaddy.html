How to cancel GoDaddy domain auto-renew

Independent guide · not affiliated with GoDaddy

Turn auto-renew off in Domain Portfolio or Renewals & Billing. The domain stays active until its current term ends.

Last verified 11 Sep 2026

Open Domain Portfolio Official domain help

Domain auto-renew (recommended)

  1. Sign in to your GoDaddy Domain Portfolio.
  2. Select the individual domain to open Domain Settings.
  3. Under the Renew card, switch the auto-renew toggle to Off.
  4. Select Continue.
  5. If the domain has Domain Protection, complete the identity check (2SV code or one-time password emailed to the account).

Changes can take up to 90 minutes to appear. The domain remains yours and usable until the current expiration date. After that it will not renew.

Other products (hosting, SSL, email, etc.)

  1. Go to Renewals & Billing → Manage Subscriptions.
  2. Select the product → Turn off Auto-Renew.
  3. Choose a reason → Continue to Cancel.
  4. Confirm the end date, then Confirm Cancel.

Source: official Turn off auto-renew help.

If you cannot see the toggle

Domain status must be Active. Pending transfer, lock, or recent changes can block the setting. Domain Protection forces an extra verification step. Bundled domains often require turning off the whole bundle.

The trap: auto-renew is on by default. Turning it off only stops the next charge; it does not delete the domain or give a refund for the current term. Domain Protection and 2SV add friction that makes people give up. Save a screenshot of the Off state and the confirmation.
