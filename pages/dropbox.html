How to cancel Dropbox

Cancel the paid plan on the web if Dropbox billed you. That downgrades you. It does not delete the account. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

Open Dropbox account Official help

Plus, Professional, or Essentials on the web

  1. Sign in at Dropbox.
  2. Open dropbox.com/account.
  3. Select Cancel plan and finish the screens.
  4. Keep the confirmation email.

Family and team plans have their own cancel pages. A trial still has to be cancelled before it converts.

If you do not see Cancel plan

The receipt merchant is Apple or Google Play. Cancel in Settings or Play subscriptions. The desktop app cannot stop that bill.

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

The trap: files above the free limit can lock after the paid term ends. Copy them out first.
