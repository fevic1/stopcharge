How to cancel McAfee

Independent guide · not affiliated with McAfee

Turn off auto-renewal in My Account. Deleting the app or product does not stop the bill.

Last verified 24 Sep 2026

Open My Account Official help

If McAfee bills you directly

  1. Go to myaccount.mcafee.com and sign in.
  2. Select Subscriptions.
  3. Next to the plan you want to stop, select Cancel.
  4. Scroll down and select Cancel Auto-Renewal.
  5. Confirm. Save any confirmation email or screen.

Your current paid term stays active until it ends. It will not renew. McAfee says turn off auto-renewal more than 30 days before the term expires to avoid the next charge.

If you bought through Apple, Google Play, or a retailer

McAfee cannot cancel a store or third-party bill. Cancel where you subscribed:

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

Retailer or device-bundle purchases: manage renewal with that seller.

Refund window

McAfee’s published path: request a refund within 30 days of initial purchase or within 60 days of an automatic renewal charge (for 1-year or longer terms). Contact Customer Service after you turn off auto-renewal if you need a refund for a recent charge.

The trap: retention screens and “you will lose benefits” warnings. Keep going until Cancel Auto-Renewal is confirmed. Uninstalling the software does nothing to the subscription.
