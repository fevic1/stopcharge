How to cancel ExpressVPN

Independent guide · not affiliated with ExpressVPN

Turn auto-renewal off in the ExpressVPN portal. Deleting the app does not cancel the bill.

Last verified 26 Sep 2026

Open My subscription Official help

If you bought directly from ExpressVPN

  1. Open any browser and go to portal.expressvpn.com/my-subscription. Log in if prompted (email + verification code).
  2. Below Automatic renewal, click Cancel subscription.
  3. Follow the prompts until you see confirmation that the subscription has been canceled.
  4. You keep access until the last day of the current paid cycle. You will not be billed for another cycle.

Source: official cancel guide (last updated 7 Aug 2026).

If you bought through Apple or Google Play

ExpressVPN cannot cancel a store bill. Cancel where you subscribed:

Apple subscriptions · Play subscriptions

After you cancel in the store, you can still use the service until the end of the current cycle.

Bitcoin or Paymentwall purchases

These do not auto-renew. No cancel step is required.

Refund window

First-time direct purchases carry a 30-day money-back guarantee. Request a refund in the portal (Profile → Account & Billing → View Payments → Request refund) or via 24/7 live chat. Store purchases (Apple/Play) are refunded only at the store’s discretion; ExpressVPN’s guarantee does not apply to them. Prior refund recipients are ineligible.

The trap: you cannot cancel inside the ExpressVPN app. Long prepaid plans auto-renew by default. People uninstall the client and assume billing stopped. The cancel lives only on the web portal (or in the app store that billed you). Save the confirmation screen and any email.
