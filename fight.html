StopCharge

They charged you anyway

Money already taken. Cancel first. Ask the merchant (or Apple) second. The bank is third. Banks ask whether you cancelled with the merchant.

Order — do not skip a step

  1. Cancel on the official path. Keep the confirmation.
  2. Save the three screenshots before you close the tab.
  3. If the statement says Apple / iTunes / App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com before any bank dispute.
  4. If the merchant billed you directly: send the refund letter from your email client.
  5. If they ignore you or refuse: dispute in the card app or PayPal. Attach the packet.
  6. Still stuck: regulator. That is pressure, not a courtroom.

Three screenshots

  1. The active plan (price, renewal date, name).
  2. The cancellation confirmed screen with date and time.
  3. The confirmation email, saved as a file.

Windows (typical, not a promise)

  • Cards: often about 120 days from the charge.
  • US billing-error letter: generally 60 days from the statement that first showed the error.
  • PayPal: often 180 days. Escalate inside 20 days.
  • Apple Report a Problem: recent charges on that Apple ID. Status often 24–48 hours.

Chargeback without burning the account

Tell the bank: cancelled recurring / services not received after cancel. Three sentences: what you bought, when you cancelled, that they billed after that date. Ask for a case number.

Skip the bank if the merchant is Apple until Report a Problem has an answer. A chargeback against Apple can freeze the Apple ID until the balance is treated as unpaid.
