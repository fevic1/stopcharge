They charged you anyway

A chargeback is not a complaint form. It is a case between your bank and the merchant’s bank, run on card network rules. This page is the packet that case needs. It is a public guide, not legal advice. Last checked 1 Sep 2026.

What the issuer is deciding

The bank is not asking whether the product annoyed you. It is asking whether the charge was unauthorized, whether the service was not provided, or whether the merchant kept billing after a completed cancel.

Recurring digital plans almost always land in “canceled but still billed” or “services not received after cancel.” Those codes fail when the only story is “I closed the app.”

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover each publish reason codes and time limits. Typical consumer windows sit around 120 days from the charge, or from the date the service should have stopped. PayPal’s Resolution Center is often about 180 days, and the case can close if you do not escalate inside about 20 days. A US billing error letter under the Fair Credit Billing Act is generally 60 days from the statement that first showed the error. Those numbers are typical, not a promise. The issuer’s own form is the rule that applies to that card.

Order. Do not skip a step

  1. Cancel on the official path for that brand. Apple billed plans die in Settings or apple.com/billing. Play billed plans die in Play subscriptions.
  2. Save the three files before you close the tab.
  3. If the statement says Apple, iTunes, or App Store: use reportaproblem.apple.com before any bank dispute.
  4. If the merchant billed you directly: send the refund letter from your own email client. Not a message to us.
  5. If they ignore you or refuse: dispute that one charge in the card app or PayPal. Attach the packet. Ask for a case number.
  6. Still stuck: a regulator. That is pressure, not a courtroom.

Issuers ask whether you canceled with the merchant. A first strike at the bank, with no cancel proof, is why most of these cases lose.

The evidence packet

Banks do not want a novel. They want a short chain a reviewer can check in two minutes.

  1. Identity of the plan: name, price, renewal date, from the active subscription screen taken before cancel.
  2. Proof the cancel completed: the cancellation confirmed screen with date and time visible.
  3. The confirmation email, saved as a file, from the address already on the account.
  4. The statement line that matches that merchant and amount.
  5. Your written demand to the merchant, and their silence or refusal.
  6. If the button was hidden: screenshots of the maze, and the receipt that shows Apple, Play, or a partner as the real biller.

Without the first three items the file is a story. The merchant’s reply will show an active plan and no cancel timestamp. The issuer then sides with the merchant.

Windows (typical, not a promise)

  • Cards: often about 120 days from the charge, or from when the service should have stopped.
  • US billing error letter: generally 60 days from the statement that first showed the error.
  • PayPal: often 180 days. Escalate inside 20 days.
  • Apple Report a Problem: recent charges on that Apple ID. Status often 24 to 48 hours.

Card networks set floors. The issuer can be stricter. Some banks want the merchant letter first. Some will take screenshots only. Some reject a recurring billing dispute filed after you kept using the service for another cycle. Read the form in your bank app. That form is the policy that applies to you.

What to tell the bank

Reason: canceled recurring charge, or services not received after cancel.

Three sentences: what you bought, when you canceled, that they billed after that date. Attach the packet. Ask for a case number.

Apple billed plans

Skip the bank until Report a Problem has an answer. A chargeback against Apple can freeze the Apple ID until the balance is treated as unpaid. Apple often grants a one time courtesy refund on that form.

reportaproblem.apple.com · Anything billed by Apple

Stop the next charge

Lock a virtual card and freeze it. Privacy.com is US only. Elsewhere: Revolut, Wise, or your bank. Some banks let you replace the plastic and opt out of card updater networks so the old merchant cannot follow the new number.

If they ignore the letter

  • US: FTC ReportFraud · CFPB
  • UK: Citizens Advice
  • EU / EEA: ECC-Net
  • Thailand: OCPB

What this page is not

We do not file the dispute for you. We do not store your dates or card numbers. We do not recite a live Visa reason code manual. That text goes stale and would read like legal advice.

The directory is the cancel map. This page is the evidence spec. The letters are the payload. The bank is a process with a deadline. If the packet is complete inside the window, you have a case. If it is not, extra wording on this site will not save it.
