For humans and for people billed by an AI / LLM product. We publish the maze. We do not refund you. No account numbers on the form.

What to file here

  • Cancel is call-only, buried, or missing after signup was one click.
  • They charged after a confirmation screen or email.
  • An AI chatbot, image tool, coding agent, or “plus” plan renewed after you thought you left.
  • A StopCharge page is stale (button renamed, URL dead).

AI / LLM subscriptions

Most of these bills sit on Apple, Google Play, Stripe, or the vendor site — not inside the chat window.

  1. If the receipt says Apple: cancel in Apple Subscriptions.
  2. If it says Google Play: cancel in Play.
  3. If it says the vendor (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini Advanced, Midjourney, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, etc.): cancel on their billing page, then freeze a virtual card.
  4. Then file the maze so the next person is not hunting the same button.
