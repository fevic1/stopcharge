StopCharge

Letters you can send today

Fill the brackets. Attach screenshots. Do not paste account numbers into a public form.

1. Refund demand to the company

Send to the billing / cancellation address they publish. Keep a copy.

Subject: Refund request — cancelled [PLAN] — charged [DATE] — [AMOUNT]

I cancelled [PLAN / MERCHANT] on [DATE TIME, timezone] through [web / app / store].
Confirmation: [email subject or confirmation ID].

You charged [AMOUNT] on [CHARGE DATE] after that cancellation.

Please refund that charge to the original payment method by [DATE + 10 days]
and confirm in writing that the subscription will not renew.

Attached: screenshot of the active plan, cancellation confirmation, and the charge line.

[NAME]
[ACCOUNT EMAIL THEY HAVE — not extra personal data]

2. Dispute notice to the bank or card issuer

Use the billing-inquiries address on the statement if you send paper. The in-app dispute is faster; this letter backs it.

Subject: Billing dispute — cancelled recurring charge — [AMOUNT] on [DATE]

I dispute the charge of [AMOUNT] on [DATE] to [MERCHANT].

I cancelled the service on [CANCEL DATE]. I was billed after cancellation.
This is a cancelled recurring transaction / unauthorized renewal.

I already asked the merchant for a refund on [DATE]. They have not refunded.

Please credit the charge and block further billing from this merchant.
Case attachments: cancellation confirmation, refund demand, statement line.

[NAME]
[LAST 4 OF CARD ONLY]

3. Dark-pattern note (optional last paragraph)

Add only if you have screenshots of the maze. Do not write “I will sue.”

The cancel control was not on the billing page. I had to pass [N] extra screens
before “End membership” / “Cancel plan” appeared. Screenshots attached.
I am also sending this record to [FTC ReportFraud / CFPB / Citizens Advice / OCPB]
if the refund is not issued by [DATE].

A letter is not a verdict. Adobe annual-paid-monthly fees and used-trial refunds follow the plan you signed. Read the brand page first.
