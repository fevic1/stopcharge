StopCharge · Mbio Team

We do not appear on your bank statement

stopcharge.net is a free cancel-path directory. There is no checkout, no account, no subscription, no payment processor. We cannot charge a card.

If a search result said we are a billing descriptor

That text is wrong. We do not run a lookup tool that takes your email or transaction ID. Do not type card numbers into a form that claims to be us.

What to do with the real line on the statement

  1. Read the merchant name on the statement, not a blog about this domain.
  2. Open that brand’s official cancel path from the directory.
  3. If the merchant is Apple or Google Play, cancel in the store, not in the app.
  4. Keep three screenshots, then use your mailbox.

We will not

  • Take a card, PayPal, or crypto.
  • Cancel a plan for you.
  • File a chargeback for you.
  • Look up a charge from your email.
